# Map every Calculator replica token

An exact behavioral and visual replica of Apple’s current iPhone Calculator cannot be built from Apple Design Resources alone: Apple documents **modes, key roles, gestures, and math affordances** in Support guides, but publishes **no Calculator-specific point sizes, hex fills, SF Symbol bindings, or keypad asset catalog**. For iOS 18+, the product is no longer a single four-row pad—it is **Basic, Scientific (including portrait), Convert, Math Notes, and History**, with expression display, unit/currency conversion, and Notes-backed handwriting math ([Get started with Calculator on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/get-started-with-calculator-iph6a38eb783/ios)). Implementers must therefore fuse **Apple-documented interaction models** with **community-measured visual tokens** (circular keys near **~75–80 pt**, orange operators via **`systemOrange` / `#FF9500`–`#FF9F0A`**, always-dark canvas) and treat digit/function fills, portrait scientific grids, press animation curves, and several math edge cases as **on-device verification gaps**. This report is a practical replica blueprint: screens and layout maps first, then visual tokens, math UX quirks, icons/platform/a11y, and IP constraints—explicitly separating official facts from reverse-engineered estimates. It is **not legal advice**.

## Five surfaces replace one keypad after iOS 18

Apple’s current Calculator is a multi-mode utility. Official paths are **basic arithmetic**, the **scientific calculator**, **unit/currency conversion**, and **Math Notes** (type or handwrite equations, variables, and graphs), selected from a mode control rather than by rotating the phone ([Get started with Calculator on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/get-started-with-calculator-iph6a38eb783/ios); [Use the scientific calculator on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-the-scientific-calculator-iphb977c4c0e/ios)). **Convert** overlays Basic or Scientific: choose from/to units or currencies, type either field for live conversion, and reverse with a swap control; the same conversion language works in Math Notes (for example `50 m in feet =`) ([Convert units or currency in Calculator on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/convert-units-or-currency-iph69d274dde/ios)). **History** applies to Basic/Scientific calculations: open history, tap an entry to reload, touch-and-hold for Copy Expression / Copy Result, swipe left to delete, and use Edit for Clear All or multi-delete; Math Notes live in Notes, not Calculator history ([See previous calculations in Calculator on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/see-previous-calculations-iph14c31d991/ios); [MacRumors — iOS 18 Calculator](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-18-calculator-app/)).

Community and review sources fix the iOS 18 chrome map that Support describes only with unlabeled icons: a **history/list control** (top-leading), a **bottom-leading calculator/mode control** opening Basic / Scientific / Math Notes plus Convert, **portrait Scientific** without landscape, **expression tape** while typing with the expression retained above the result after `=`, and a **dedicated Delete/backspace** replacing the old swipe-on-display digit delete ([Gadget Hacks — iOS 18 Calculator features](https://ios.gadgethacks.com/how-to/all-must-try-new-features-make-apples-iphone-calculator-force-be-reckoned-with-0385567/); [MacStories — iOS/iPadOS 18 Review](https://www.macstories.net/stories/ios-and-ipados-18-the-macstories-review/6/); [MacRumors — iOS 18 Calculator](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-18-calculator-app/)). **Pre-iOS 18**, Scientific appeared only in landscape and portrait returned Basic ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/22197381/apple-ios-calculator-scientific-mode-landscape); [How to Open the Hidden Scientific Calculator on iPhone](https://www.howtogeek.com/707141/how-to-open-the-hidden-scientific-calculator-on-iphone/)). **iPadOS 18** ships first-party Calculator with the same feature set plus Apple Pencil Math Notes and multitasking ([Apple Newsroom — iPadOS 18](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2024/06/ipados-18-introduces-powerful-intelligence-features-and-apps-for-apple-pencil/)). Control Center can host a Calculator **control** that launches the app (including from Lock Screen when Control Center is allowed when locked); long-press copying the last result is reported for that control, but there is no confirmed first-party Lock Screen **keypad widget** ([Cult of Mac](https://www.cultofmac.com/how-to/calculator-app-inside-control-center); [WWDC24 — Extend your app’s controls across the system](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2024/10157/)).

**Apple-documented vs community.** Modes, Convert, History actions, Math Notes solve/graph/variable rules, and Scientific key *roles* are Apple-documented. Exact History chrome (sidebar vs bottom sheet—Gadget Hacks notes an iOS 18.1 bottom-sheet tweak), StandBy/Dynamic Island surfaces, and Settings → Apps → Calculator prefs depth are community or incomplete. For Math Notes specifically, Apple documents Insert Results / Suggest Results / Off, value scrubbing, red dotted underlines for unsolvable cases (including divide-by-zero), blue dotted for ambiguous handwriting, Latin variable names, and 2D/3D graph insert rules ([Solve math with Math Notes](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/solve-math-with-math-notes-iph46efa613a/ios); [Work with graphs in Math Notes](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/work-with-graphs-in-math-notes-iph09ad8b507/ios); [How to handle errors in Math Notes](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120852)). Math Notes is Calculator/Notes math solving—not branded as Apple Intelligence—even though iOS 18 ships Intelligence elsewhere ([Apple Newsroom — iOS 18](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2024/06/ios-18-makes-iphone-more-personal-capable-and-intelligent-than-ever/); [MacRumors — Notes iOS 18](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-18-notes-app/)). An iPhone replica should **omit macOS-only Programmer and RPN** modes ([Choose the right mode in Calculator on Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-sa/guide/calculator/calc22d50970/mac); [Get started with Calculator on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/get-started-with-calculator-iph6a38eb783/ios)).

## Layout grids and geometry converge on circles, not published points

Apple publishes a **44×44 pt** minimum hit-region floor for buttons, not Calculator diameters, gutters, or display baselines ([Apple HIG — Buttons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/buttons)). The implementable grid therefore rests on documented structure plus reverse-engineered maps.

### Scientific keys Apple documents (roles and a few positions)

Apple lists memory **`m+` / `m-` / `mr` / `mc`**; powers and logs including **`x²` `x³` `xʸ` `eˣ` `10ˣ` `x!` `1/x` `EE`** plus inverses (roots, `ln`, `log`) via the row below / **`2nd`**; trig and hyperbolic **`sin` `cos` `tan` `sinh` `cosh` `tanh`** with **`2nd`** inverses; constants **`π` `ℯ`**; **`Rand`** in [0, 1]; and **degrees default** with **`Rad`/`Deg`** toggle. Portrait placements Apple states: **Rad/Deg just above `÷`**, **Rand just above `.`**; landscape: Rad/Deg near bottom center, Rand bottom left ([Use the scientific calculator on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-the-scientific-calculator-iphb977c4c0e/ios)). Portrait Scientific stacks function keys **above** the basic pad ([Gadget Hacks](https://ios.gadgethacks.com/how-to/all-must-try-new-features-make-apples-iphone-calculator-force-be-reckoned-with-0385567/); [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-18-calculator-app/)). Apple does **not** publish a full labeled portrait Scientific grid or a complete `2nd` alternate legend.

### Geometry estimates suitable for implementation

Designers commonly solve **`D = (W − 2M − (C−1)G) / C`** with **C = 4**, margins **M ≈ 12–20 pt**, gutters **G ≈ 10–16 pt**—on a **393 pt** width with M=16 and G=14 that yields **D ≈ 79.75 pt**; open-source clones often hard-code **80×80** circles and **16 pt** spacing; corner radius is **D/2** (perfect circles), with legacy wide zero as capsule **`2D + G`** ([EveryCalculators iPhone Calculator Design](https://calculator.everycalculators.com/iphone-calculator-design/); [Liquid-Calculator](https://github.com/0xatrilla/Liquid-Calculator)). Pin the keypad above the **home-indicator safe area**; leave flexible space under the status bar / Dynamic Island—Apple does not document Calculator-specific Island insets ([EveryCalculators](https://calculator.everycalculators.com/iphone-calculator-design/)).

**Display behavior (version-gated).** Community reports for **≤ iOS 17**: roughly **9** portrait / **16** landscape digits before scientific notation, plus **swipe on the display** to delete digits ([Gadget Hacks](https://ios.gadgethacks.com/how-to/all-must-try-new-features-make-apples-iphone-calculator-force-be-reckoned-with-0385567/); [How-To Geek — swipe delete digits](https://www.howtogeek.com/262640/quickly-delete-digits-in-ios-calculator-with-a-swipe/)). For **iOS 18+**: full expression while typing, expression above result after `=`, horizontal **pan** for overflow, Delete key instead of swipe-delete, and less aggressive forcing of scientific notation ([Gadget Hacks](https://ios.gadgethacks.com/how-to/all-must-try-new-features-make-apples-iphone-calculator-force-be-reckoned-with-0385567/)). Clones commonly pair large right-aligned type with **`minimumScaleFactor` / `adjustsFontSizeToFitWidth`**; Apple’s own display point sizes are unpublished ([Apple docs — minimumScaleFactor](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/view/minimumscalefactor(_:))). Convert category inventories in press (~15 categories, ~105 currencies / ~110 units) exceed Apple’s illustrative Support lists—treat the full unit table as **verify on device** ([Gadget Hacks](https://ios.gadgethacks.com/how-to/all-must-try-new-features-make-apples-iphone-calculator-force-be-reckoned-with-0385567/); [Convert units or currency](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/convert-units-or-currency-iph69d274dde/ios)).

### Glyphs: SF Symbols vs text

Apple documents SF Symbols for mathematical operators (`multiply` and variants, etc.) and catalogs names such as **`plus` `minus` `multiply` `divide` `equal` `percent` `plusminus` / `plus.forwardslash.minus` `function` `delete.left`** ([Configuring and displaying symbol images](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/configuring-and-displaying-symbol-images-in-your-ui); [SF Symbols](https://developer.apple.com/sf-symbols/); [sf-symbols-online](https://github.com/andrewtavis/sf-symbols-online)). Apple does **not** confirm stock Calculator uses those symbols versus Unicode text. Practical replica mapping used by high-fidelity clones: **SF Symbols for operators/±/%/=**, **Text for digits and decimal**, avoid ASCII `x` for multiply ([Medium SwiftUI clone](https://medium.com/@hendralijaya/tutorial-clone-calculator-ios-swiftui-part-1-cloning-the-ui-a35f72ce7378)). History “three lines” and mode “calculator” glyphs are unlabeled in Support—verify names in the SF Symbols app (`line.3.horizontal` / `list.bullet` are common approximations, not confirmed internals) ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-18-calculator-app/); [Apple Support — Math Notes](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/solve-math-with-math-notes-iph46efa613a/ios)).

**Press interaction.** Community analysis holds that Calculator keys are **custom drawings with touch tracking** (highlight follows finger across keys), not stock `UIButton` chrome ([Stack Overflow — Calculator highlight](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/62856110/how-does-apple-do-the-uibutton-highlight-effect-in-the-calculator-app)). Momentary press is often lighten/opacity; sticky selected operator is commonly **inverted orange**. Exact fill hexes, springs, and durations remain unpublished.

## Math UX quirks decide whether the replica feels like Apple

**Documented Basic/Scientific behavior.** Scientific uses **standard order of operations** with parentheses for grouping and full expressions before `=` ([Use the scientific calculator](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-the-scientific-calculator-iphb977c4c0e/ios)). Current Support documents **repeating `=`** to re-apply the last binary operation (`1 × 2 =` → 2, then further `=` → 4, 8, 16…) and **last-operation reuse** on a new operand (`100 + 15% =` → 115; enter `150` then `=` → **172.5**) ([Use the basic calculator](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-the-basic-calculator-iph1ac0b5cc/ios)). That Support text **contradicts** iOS 18 launch reporting that claimed `=` became “final” and stopped repeating—**device-verify on the target build** ([Gadget Hacks](https://ios.gadgethacks.com/how-to/all-must-try-new-features-make-apples-iphone-calculator-force-be-reckoned-with-0385567/)).

**Percent.** Apple’s worked examples treat `%` as a last-operand modifier for tip/discount style with `+`/`−`. Industry analysis of pocket-calculator percent (including Apple-class machines) models `A ± B%` as replacing the second operand with `A × B / 100` ([Raymond Chen — How does the calculator percent key work?](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/oldnewthing/20080110-00/?p=23853)). Community reports that **iOS 18 stopped showing the intermediate percentage amount** on `%` press (pre-18 often showed the amount before `=`), producing user confusion on sequences like `100 − 75%` ([Apple Community](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/255778039); [Apple Community — Percentages](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/255796027)). **`%` with `×`/`÷` and deep chains** has no official Apple algorithm—Chen’s model is the best public description, not Apple documentation. Historic iPhone vs Mac `%` chaining divergences appear largely resolved on newer macOS in community answers ([Ask Different](https://apple.stackexchange.com/questions/214839/how-to-solve-the-issue-of-on-calculator-giving-different-results-on-iphone-v)).

**Precision and errors.** Apple does not publish Calculator’s numeric engine. Historical Engadget accuracy suites on older iOS generally passed common cases; iOS 18 community comments still report float-like quirks such as `1 ÷ 9` then `× 9` → `0.99999999` ([Engadget — calculator accuracy](https://www.engadget.com/2013-11-12-calculating-the-difference-a-look-at-calculator-accuracy-in-ios.html); [MacRumors comments](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-18-calculator-app/)). `NSDecimalNumber` is Apple’s currency-safe decimal type for **implementers**, not a claim about stock internals ([NSDecimalNumber archive](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/LegacyTechnologies/WebObjects/WebObjects_3.5/Reference/Frameworks/ObjC/Foundation/Classes/NSDecimalNumber/Description.html)). Keypad divide-by-zero is reported as textual **“divide by zero”** (not ∞) on recent builds, while Math Notes uses red dotted underlines; `0÷0` messaging varies by era ([How to handle errors in Math Notes](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120852); community tester reports). Long-press copies results/history; shake-to-undo remains disabled per community ([Use the basic calculator](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-the-basic-calculator-iph1ac0b5cc/ios); [Gadget Hacks](https://ios.gadgethacks.com/how-to/all-must-try-new-features-make-apples-iphone-calculator-force-be-reckoned-with-0385567/)).

## Icons, chrome, haptics, a11y, localization, and IP hard stops

**App icon.** The Calculator Home Screen icon has been redesigned across nearly every OS era—from skeuomorphic faces (iPhone OS 1.x–iOS 6) through flat four-key tiles (iOS 7–11) and full-calculator glyphs (iOS 11–17) to an **iOS 18** tweak replacing the large bottom grey key with **two smaller keys**, with further Liquid Glass–era revisions catalogued for later betas ([Logopedia: Calculator (iOS)](https://logos.fandom.com/wiki/Calculator_(iOS)); [Jim Nielsen — Short History of iOS App Icons](https://blog.jim-nielsen.com/2022/short-history-of-ios-app-icons/); [Basic Apple Guy — iOS Icon History](https://basicappleguy.com/basicappleblog/ios-icon-history)). System masking applies a squircle; replicas should ship a **full-bleed square original asset**, never a pre-rounded extracted Apple PNG ([Jim Nielsen](https://blog.jim-nielsen.com/2022/short-history-of-ios-app-icons/)).

**In-app chrome.** Expect a **visible status bar with light content** over black UI, **no traditional navigation bar title**, safe-area home indicator, edge-to-edge dark canvas, history overlay, and mode control ([TN3105 — status bar style](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technotes/tn3105-customizing-uistatusbar-syle); [Gadget Hacks — color mods](https://ios.gadgethacks.com/how-to/update-your-iphone-calculators-look-with-these-easy-color-mods-0384541/)). App Store categorization places Calculator under **Utilities**; App Library clustering follows system rules users cannot manually override ([App Store — Calculator](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/calculator/id1069511488); [Apple Support — App Library](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/find-and-use-your-apps-in-app-library-iph87abad19a/ios)).

**Haptics and sound.** No Apple-published Calculator key-click or Taptic specification exists. Jailbreak-era tweaks and third-party calculators market haptics as an **addition**, implying stock taps are traditionally silent/haptic-free ([iDownloadBlog — EinsteinVibes](https://www.idownloadblog.com/2018/08/16/einsteinvibes/); [Calcora](https://calcora.app/); [MacRumors — iOS 18 Calculator](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-18-calculator-app/)). For stock fidelity, default to **no** `UIImpactFeedbackGenerator` and **no** key audio; optional light impact is a deliberate UX deviation ([HIG — Playing haptics](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/playing-haptics)).

**Localization.** Use locale-aware decimal and grouping separators via `NumberFormatter` / `Locale` (period vs comma) ([Internationalizing Locale Data](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/MacOSX/Conceptual/BPInternational/InternationalizingLocaleData/InternationalizingLocaleData.html)). Math Notes documents Western Arabic numerals plus typed **Arabic (Eastern & Western)** and **Devanagari** with regional availability caveats; variable names remain Latin ([Solve math with Math Notes](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/solve-math-with-math-notes-iph46efa613a/ios); [WWDC22 — Get it right (to left)](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2022/10107/)). Whether the **basic keypad digits** themselves relabel to Eastern Arabic-Indic forms is not confirmed in Support—verify per region. Prefer mathematical symbols for operators (usually unmirrored) and RTL-aware leading/trailing for history/backspace chrome; SF Symbols can adapt reading direction ([SF Symbols](https://developer.apple.com/sf-symbols/)).

**Accessibility.** Follow HIG VoiceOver labeling for glyph-only controls; a third-party accessibility write-up targeting Apple Calculator parity recommends **`.keyboardKey` / `.isKeyboardKey`** traits and spoken operator names ([HIG — VoiceOver](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/voiceover); [Keeping Score! — VoiceOver](https://www.keepingscore.app/docs/accessibility/voiceover/)). Announce display changes with accessibility notifications; ensure Switch Control can reach every key. Stock Calculator’s Dynamic Type reflow, Increase Contrast interaction beyond orange shift, and official a11y audit results are unpublished—visual-fidelity replicas often keep fixed key geometry while enlarging the readout.

**IP and distribution constraints (not legal advice).** Apple’s trademark/copyright guidelines prohibit using Apple-owned graphic symbols, logos, or **icons** and imitating distinctive Apple packaging/website design/logos/typefaces without license ([Guidelines for Using Apple Trademarks and Copyrights](https://www.apple.com/legal/intellectual-property/guidelinesfor3rdparties.html)). App Review **Guideline 4.1 Copycats** rejects near-identical UI/name/icon impersonation ([App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/); [Apple Developer Forums — Preventing Copycats](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/782175)). Safer practice: original app name/icon assets, SF Symbols via public APIs, genre-level four-function layout, and “inspired by” framing—**never** extracted Calculator PNGs/PDFs from IPSW or app bundles, never “Apple Calculator” branding. Personal/portfolio use lowers App Store rejection risk but does **not** erase copyright or trade-dress exposure.

## Conclusion

Pixel-faithful Calculator work is a **hybrid engineering problem**: Apple already published enough of the **interaction grammar** (modes, Scientific roles, Convert, History, Math Notes, clear/copy gestures, order-of-operations Scientific) that a replica’s architecture should be multi-surface from day one, while the **visual and metric layer**—exact iOS 18 function-key hex, portrait Scientific cell map, display point sizes, press springs, and several percent/`=` edge cases—still requires **screenshot measurement on a pinned OS build**. The strategic implication for builders is to version-lock the target (iOS 18 Delete-era vs current Support C/AC+backspace vs later Liquid Glass icon language), implement semantic orange and forced-dark chrome, and treat community hex/grid tables as calibrated starting points rather than Apple specs. Doing that separation up front is what turns “looks like Calculator” into a maintainable parity project instead of an unsourced pixel chase—and what keeps distribution on the right side of Apple’s copycat and trademark rules.
